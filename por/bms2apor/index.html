Status

  • 1 and 2 rows: translated by rules (experimental). 3 rows and more: not supported yet.
  • Standard matrices are assumed; results for non-standard input are not guaranteed.

Output

An additive pattern of resemblance of order 1 (Carlson), written as a list of nodes from left to right, and the node that denotes the ordinal (point).

  • 0 is the node 0. Letters a, b, … are additively indecomposable nodes, named in increasing order.
  • b+a is the decomposable node b + a.
  • (x … y) is a ≤₁-circle: x ≤₁ z for every node z from x to y.

Example: 0 a (b b+b) point: b denotes ε₀.

Method

The matrix is read as a term of Buchholz's notation (p進大好きbot's correspondence), and the pattern is the closure of {0, α} under additive decomposition, the ≤₁-reach and the preceding point, in the spirit of Carlson and Wilken's normal forms for elementary patterns. The ≤₁-reach of a point is computed from the images of its argument, with levels lowered inside collapses.

Checks

Not a proof. Order preservation was checked on all 44653 standard 2-row matrices with at most 8 columns: consecutive matrices in BMS order give strictly increasing patterns, compared with the amalgamation algorithm of poral (S. Alexander).

Input

  • One matrix per line; all lines are translated.
  • Characters other than (),0-9 are ignored. Shorter columns are padded with 0.
  • The input is mirrored to the URL query ?bms=.
